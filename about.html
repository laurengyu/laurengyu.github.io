About

Welcome—This is Lauren. Lovely to meet you here.

I’m a product data scientist. I design and analyze experiments, use causal inference to separate what actually works from what merely correlates, and dig into data to understand why users behave the way they do. At heart, it’s work that runs on curiosity.

When I’m not working, I work on chess puzzles, write fiction, and spend more time than I’d like to admit looking up the ingredients behind my skincare.
