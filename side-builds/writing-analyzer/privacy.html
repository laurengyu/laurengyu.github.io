Privacy

Prose Analyzer · last updated October 2026

The short version: your text is analyzed and then forgotten. I don’t keep it, read it, or share it.

What happens to your text

  • When you click “Draw the portrait”, your text is sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to a small server on Google Cloud Run.
  • The server counts and tags the words, sends the numbers back to your browser, and discards the text. It is never written to disk, a database or a log.
  • The server’s logs record only that a request happened and how many words it had, never the words themselves.

What I don’t do

  • No accounts, no cookies, no analytics or tracking scripts on this page.
  • Your text is not used to train any AI model, and it is not shared with anyone.

Third parties

The page is hosted on GitHub Pages and the analysis runs on Google Cloud. Like any web host, they may keep standard technical logs (such as IP addresses) under their own privacy policies. Fonts load from Google Fonts. The “Buy me a coffee” link goes to buymeacoffee.com, which has its own policy.
